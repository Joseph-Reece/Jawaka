[ PHOTO: depot / team with farmer ]

Our story

Jawaka Distributors started with a simple observation: farmers were buying feed, but nobody was helping them use it well. We built our business around fixing that — sourcing feed from manufacturers we trust, and pairing every bag with real technical support.

Today we supply broiler farmers, layer farmers, and first-time poultry keepers across Nyeri and beyond, with feeds from Backbone, Suguna, Isinya, and Sigma, plus day-old chicks from Kenchic, Suguna, and Isinya.

Our Mission
Help every farmer we serve raise healthier birds and run a more profitable poultry business.
Our Vision
To be the most trusted poultry feeds and chicks partner for farmers across Kenya.
Our Promise
Genuine products, honest advice, and a team that picks up the phone when you need us.
